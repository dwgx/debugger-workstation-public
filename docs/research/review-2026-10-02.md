**Status of the fixes:** the defects below were measured against the tree at commit

## 0. The technique that found all of it: ablation

Six of the seven findings in this document exist because one method was applied: **delete
the mechanism, require the existing suite to go red.** A suite that stays green after you
remove a component proves the component is untested, not that it is correct.

The primary source, quoted in `w1-agentworkflow.md:64-76`:

> **Source:** `anthropics/skills` — `skills/claude-api/shared/evals/eval-audit.md` §5,
> "Can it detect the change you're after?" (raw at
> `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/claude-api/shared/evals/eval-audit.md`),
> cited `w1-agentworkflow.md:64-69`:
> *"The mechanism is wired. Whatever the score is supposed to depend on — a tool, a memory
> store, an instruction file — disable it and confirm the score drops; enable it and confirm
> it engages in the transcripts. If the score barely moves either way, the eval isn't
> measuring the lever you plan to pull."*
>
> **Corroboration:** `anthropic.com/engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps`, cited
> `w1-agentworkflow.md:70-73`: *"every component in a harness encodes an assumption about
> what the model can't do on its own, and those assumptions are worth stress testing, both
> because they may be incorrect, and because they can quickly go stale as models improve."*
>
> **Restated as a rule** (`w1-agentworkflow.md:75-76`): for each component, remove it and
> require the existing test suite to turn red.

The suite applies the same bar to the *validator*'s rules: a rule is load-bearing only if a
selftest case points at it. `w1-agentworkflow.md:919-921` records the driver and its result:
10 of 11 rule functions went red under ablation; **`r_shape` stayed green.**

**Why this document leads with the method and not the findings.** Every defect below is a
statement about a component that the estate already described as enforced. Reading would
not have found most of them — `gate.ps1`'s selftest printed `GATE SELFTEST OK: 10
refusals and grants all behaved.` (`w1-gateredteam.md:38`) while a grant forged from
nothing passed with `exit=0` (`w1-gateredteam.md:101`). Only mutation separates "the code
is right" from "nothing checks the code."

**Also measured, and the reason the method generalises here:** the instrument, not the
subject, was wrong 13 times in this project's recorded history, and again in this round —
`gate.ps1`'s selftest reads `$g3.code` while `New-Grant` returns keys
`ok, grant, allowed_count, exit`, so the vacuity guard fires on every run and is wired to
nothing (`w1-gateredteam.md:451-461`).

Three of these deserve their mechanism spelled out, because each is a *label* standing in
for a *measurement*:

**`independence` counts strings, not instruments** (`w1-gateredteam.md:278-296`).
`$famOf[$c.id] = $c.evidence_source` — the gate counts **distinct `evidence_source` strings**.
IDA Pro carries two: `native.ida.decompile` = `decompiler`,
`native.ida.list_functions` = `static-analysis`. Output: *"2 capabilities across 2 evidence
families: decompiler, static-analysis"*. Same binary, same install, same session. The
gate's own comment says the point is "about the FAMILY of instrument, not the tool id", and
then implements it as a count of per-capability decorator strings the registry chooses.

**`discharged` never checks that the guard ran** (`w1-gateredteam.md:297-310`). `$dischargedOk`
checked three things: ids exist, `state` is `observed`, and a non-empty list when there are
no findings. It **never checked the named guard appears in `$Evidence`.** Naming any
observed capability discharges it. The 2026-09-30 fix correctly stopped absent/blocked ids
from discharging — *"the same defect one level over"*.

**`bounded_claim` is a five-token keyword test** (`w1-gateredteam.md:312-331`).
`$hasLimits` is `-match '(?i)\bbut\b|\bhowever\b|\bexcept\b|\bdoes not prove\b|\blimitation\b'`.
Any of those tokens anywhere in the claim passes it; the catastrophic claim is refused only
if it also matches the `$UNBOUNDED` adverb list. The gate counts `does_not_prove` entries
into its message and compares them to nothing, and `$limits` is still computed at
`mission.ps1:578-581` and unused.

**The caller supplies the registry** (`w1-gateredteam.md:344-358`): the same evidence,
flipped `state` values, produced `failed=observation,independence` against the real
registry and `failed=independence` against a caller-supplied one. A capability declared
`blocked` became admissible evidence because the caller pointed the gate at a different
table.
